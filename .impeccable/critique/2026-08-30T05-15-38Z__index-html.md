## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment: Specific.** This is an authored surface, not a category skin. The nocturnal field station concept is carried end-to-end: the 44px measurement grid and circular specimen aperture, the sans-explains/mono-measures two-voice type system, the single amber reserved for living readouts, the single grounding shadow on the chamber alone, and the pill status vocabulary (ready/draft/grown/recovery). An unrelated product could not drop this in unchanged — the chamber instrumentation and plant reading are product-particular. The nearest-to-generic zone is the masthead + two-card split itself; everything that decorates that structure is own-world.

**Deterministic scan.** CLI scan of `index.html` (exit 2): 1 advisory — `design-system-color: rgb(0, 0, 0) on p "LOCAL GROWTH STUDY · 001"`. Verified false positive: the page loads its CSS via `src/main.ts` (`import "./styles.css"`), so the markup-only scan sees no stylesheet; the computed color in the live page is `rgb(169, 217, 174)` (Lichen Flare). In-page detector (injected into the running Vite app): **17 anti-patterns** — 11 × `undersized-ui-text` (10.88px mono eyebrows/labels/readouts, below the 11px floor), 1 × `tiny-text` (11.2px amber environment line), 3 × `kicker-above-heading` (mono eyebrows above h2s), 1 × `radial-spotlight-glow` (chamber moss glow), 1 × `codex-grid-background` (chamber measurement grid), 1 × `cramped-padding` (chamber-footer, 0px vertical padding mitigated by its 52px min-height). The kicker/glow/grid findings are all documented, intentional DESIGN.md signatures — the detector's slop-tells heuristics firing on a deliberate visual language. The detector caught what the LLM review underweighted: the 0.68rem mono floor lands at 10.88px, tripping the size floor on 11 elements plus the 11.2px environment line — a real cluster, not noise.

**Visual overlays.** Injection ran successfully in a real (headless) Chromium page: preflight mutation passed, `http://localhost:8400/detect.js` injected into the live Vite app, console reported `[impeccable] 17 anti-patterns found`, and 17 overlay elements (16 visible) rendered in the page; evidence screenshot captured. Because the browser was headless and is now closed, there is no persistent `[Human]` tab with live overlays — the screenshot is the evidence of record.

## Overall Impression

An unusually disciplined instrument: the chrome is coherent, quiet, and own-world, and the accessibility engineering is genuinely ahead of typical output. But the specimen — the entire point of the product — is the quietest thing on the page. Thin stems, tiny leaves, and low chroma against the dark ground make every plant read as a sparse sapling, while the report insists on "balanced branching, medium leaf density." The single biggest opportunity: make the plant itself the loudest, most confident element in the chamber.

## What's Working

1. **A design system that is actually enforced in code.** The amber-living-readout rule, the single chamber shadow, hairline-first structure, and the two typographic voices are not just documented in DESIGN.md — they are visibly implemented in `src/styles.css` and hold across every breakpoint inspected (1440, 900, 390).
2. **Accessibility as a product promise, executed.** Native range inputs with explicit labels + live `output` values, `aria-live` politely silenced during slider ticks and restored for grow/reset, canvas `role="img"` with a synchronized textual fallback, landmarks and a clean H1→H2 heading structure, 2px focus-visible outlines with offset, 44px slider / 48px button targets (verified in live DOM), and a global `prefers-reduced-motion` kill switch.
3. **Honest copy in the product's voice.** "This stylized visual interpretation shows…" and "Adjust the atmosphere, then grow a new specimen when you are ready" teach the loop in one sentence each without pretending botanical accuracy — exactly the calm-scientific-wonder register the brief promised.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] The specimen underwhelms at first read — the product's peak moment lands flat.**
   Why it matters: peak-end rule — the grow click is the emotional peak of the loop, and the result reads as a thin, pale sapling even at "balanced/medium" settings (63 segments, 32 leaves at defaults, but stem widths taper to <1px and leaves are ~16 model px). Users judge the whole product by this instant.
   Fix: recalibrate visual weight — thicker stem-width curve (raise the 0.68 taper floor), larger leaf sizes, stronger accent-color usage on leaf edges, and richer per-environment palettes; consider a ~600ms stems-extending grow animation (suppressed under reduced motion) so cause-and-effect is witnessed, not just delivered.
   Suggested command: `$impeccable bolder`

2. **[P1] The report contradicts the pixels — trust erodes exactly where the product explains itself.**
   Why it matters: the reading claims "balanced branching / medium leaf density" while the canvas shows a sparse Y-shaped sapling; in one grown sample the text said "medium" while roughly a dozen tiny leaves were visible. Cause-and-effect learning — the stated success criterion — breaks when the words don't match the picture.
   Fix: recalibrate label thresholds (`getHeightLabel`/`getBranchingLabel`/`getLeafDensityLabel` in `src/generator.ts`) against what the renderer actually draws, or express traits as tick-meter readouts (mono instrument style) rather than bare words, so "medium" occupies a visible mid-scale position.
   Suggested command: `$impeccable clarify`

3. **[P2] Desktop composition is unbalanced: the rail outruns the chamber by ~500px.**
   Why it matters: at 1440×900 the control rail is 1246px tall while the chamber column is 742px, leaving a large dead ground under the chamber and a ragged page bottom — the "one instrument" illusion breaks at the silhouette level. Related layout gap: on mobile (390px) the plant reading is last in DOM order, so after Grow the updated report sits below the fold and first-timers may never see it.
   Fix: let the chamber column stretch to the rail's height (canvas region absorbs it) or move the plant reading to a lower full-width reading strip; on mobile, bring the reading directly under the chamber (or add an inline trait summary there).
   Suggested command: `$impeccable layout`

4. **[P2] Micro-text: 12 elements below the 11px legibility floor.**
   Why it matters: the 0.68rem mono voice renders at 10.88px — eyebrows, status tag, chamber footer labels, local note, and all four trait terms trip the floor — and the amber environment line renders at 11.2px; on a dark ground this is a low-vision hazard and contradicts the product's own accessibility promise.
   Fix: raise the mono floor to 0.70–0.72rem (≥11.2px), bump the reading-environment line to 0.76rem, and re-check the range hints (currently 9.3px — well under any floor).
   Suggested command: `$impeccable typeset`

5. **[P2] No in-session variation memory — the compare loop runs entirely in the user's head.**
   Why it matters: PRODUCT.md defines success as creating "several coherent variations quickly" and comparing them; today each Grow erases the previous specimen, and the seed is never shown, so a liked result is unrecoverable and comparison is working-memory-only (the one cognitive-load checklist soft failure).
   Fix: session-only affordances that respect the no-persistence constraint — a small "last specimens" ghost strip (3 faint thumbnails) in the chamber footer region, plus a visible seed readout (mono, e.g. `SPECIMEN / #5EED1234`) so results are nameable.
   Suggested command: `$impeccable polish`

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (Power User)**: Keyboard flow works (Tab → sliders → buttons; Enter grows; focus retained on the pressed button) — good. But there are no accelerators at all: no environment presets, no randomize, no seed entry, no shortcut to re-grow. Every variation means hand-dragging four sliders, and a liked specimen cannot be reproduced because the seed is hidden. Alex will grow three plants and leave.

**Sam (Accessibility-Dependent User)**: The foundation is strong (native semantics, live-region discipline, canvas fallback, visible focus). Red flags that remain: 12 elements render below 11px (the entire mono measurement voice and the amber environment line), range-hints sit at ~9.3px, and trait updates are announced only via the status message — the reading-list values themselves change silently for screen-reader users mid-flow.

**Jordan (First-Timer)**: The loop line ("Change the atmosphere. Grow what it becomes.") plus populated initial state means Jordan can succeed — but "seeded output" (masthead), "MODEL / SEEDED BRANCHING" (chamber footer), and a hidden seed concept assume vocabulary Jordan doesn't have, and after tapping Grow on a phone, the reassuring reading is below the fold, so the moment of "did it work?" is answered only by a small status tag at the top.

## Minor Observations

- `theme-color` meta is `#0d1713` but the body ground is `#0b1410` — a one-value drift.
- Axis marks are asymmetric: top/bottom/left exist, right does not (neither DOM nor CSS) — reads as an oversight in an otherwise symmetrical instrument.
- `cursor: wait` on disabled buttons implies waiting, but generation is synchronous — the disabled state barely ever appears; the cursor choice can confuse.
- The `ready` status tag is the only state without a background tint (draft/grown/recovery all have one) — subtle inconsistency, defensible as "idle = quietest."
- Detector's `kicker-above-heading` (×3), `radial-spotlight-glow`, and `codex-grid-background` findings are intentional DESIGN.md signatures; noted as slop-tells only if the eyebrow pattern starts accumulating elsewhere.
- The `cramped-padding` advisory on `.chamber-footer` is mitigated by its 52px min-height — borderline false positive.

## Questions to Consider

- What if the plant grew in ~600ms before your eyes — stems extending, leaves unfurling — instead of appearing fully formed? Would cause-and-effect land twice as fast?
- What if the last three specimens haunted the chamber as faint ghost traces behind the current one — comparison without a gallery, history without persistence?
- What if trait readouts were tick-meters on the measurement grid (mono, amber, mid-scale) instead of bare words — would "medium" finally match what the eye sees?
- What does the most spectacular specimen this generator can produce look like at humidity 95 — and why isn't something close to that the first thing a visitor grows?
