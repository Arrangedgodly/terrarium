## Overall Impression

This is now a genuinely good instrument. The grow moment — old specimen receding into a trace while the new one rises over ~600ms — is the strongest authored interaction on the page, and it teaches the ghost mechanic by itself. The reading strip is truthful, the composition is balanced to the pixel, and the system discipline survived six refinement passes without dilution. The biggest remaining opportunity is efficiency: the loop's repeat rate is the product's core pleasure, and it still costs a full pointer round-trip per iteration. No blocking, major, or clustered-material issues remain.

## What's Working

1. **The witnessed grow + ghost recede.** Motion, mechanism, and meaning aligned — the chamber literally remembers. Mid-grow frame reads as appealing in-progress growth; restart and reduced-motion paths are engineered.
2. **Truthful instrumentation.** The 9,000-plant recalibration means the log never lies; verified live at both extremes tested.
3. **Composition and rhythm.** Shared-baseline columns (925px each), the full-width log strip, and the two-voice type system give the page a settled proportion at every breakpoint tested.

## Priority Issues

- **[P2] The repeat loop has no accelerator path** (the only heuristic stuck at 2)
  **Why it matters:** the product's core pleasure is rapid iteration, yet every grow costs a mouse trip to the rail; no keyboard shortcut; a specimen that mattered can be named but never re-grown.
  **Fix:** a keyboard accelerator for Grow (documented single-key or focused-button Enter flow with visible cue), and/or a compact "grow again" affordance near the chamber. Seed re-entry stays out per MVP scope.
  **Suggested command:** `$impeccable optimize`
- **[P3] Ghost traces are unexplained, and scale inversion can momentarily confuse**
  **Why it matters:** first-timers must infer what the faint plants are; when a large remembered specimen ghosts behind a much smaller live one, the ghost's silhouette outweighs the live footprint (observed post-reset).
  **Fix:** one plain-language line naming the traces; consider extra dimming when the ghost's extent exceeds the live specimen's.
  **Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`
- **[P3] Mobile reading strip loses the instrument's crispness**
  **Why it matters:** the amber environment line wraps mid-phrase (verified 2 lines at 390px) and the stacked strip runs 354px vs 165px on desktop.
  **Fix:** balanced breaking at the "·" separators (or a deliberate two-line lockup), tightened stacked spacing.
  **Suggested command:** `$impeccable typeset`
